Why Scaton EHR

Software that gets out of the way of patient care.

Your team shouldn't have to fight the system to do their jobs. Scaton EHR removes the friction between registration, consultation, diagnostics, dispensing, and payment — so every step stays connected to the same patient story.

Patient records

One patient, one journey

Registration, consultations, test results, prescriptions, and payment history all live under a single patient record — no more duplicated files.

Billing

Charges follow the care

Services rendered in the lab, pharmacy, or consultation room flow straight into the invoice — accurate, traceable, and reconciled automatically.

Operations

Fit for your facility

Whether you run a small clinic or a multi-branch hospital, configure modules, users, and workflows to match how you actually work.

Access control

Every role, the right tools

Doctors, nurses, pharmacists, lab techs, receptionists, and administrators each get a workspace built for their responsibilities — nothing more, nothing less.

Features

Everything your facility needs, connected.

From the front desk to the finance office — one platform that keeps the entire patient journey in view.

Patient Management

Register patients, store clinical history, manage appointments, and access complete records from anywhere — all in a single searchable database.

Clinical Workbench

Doctors and clinical officers get queue visibility, patient history, structured notes, diagnosis capture, and one-click lab or pharmacy requests.

Laboratory

Receive orders, track samples, record results, and release reports — with automatic charges pushed to the patient's invoice.

Pharmacy

Manage inventory, dispense prescriptions, track stock movement, and flag low or expiring medications before they become a problem.

Billing & Payments

Generate invoices tied to the visit, accept M-Pesa and cash, track outstanding balances, and reconcile every shilling against the care delivered.

Reports & Analytics

Live dashboards for patient flow, revenue, staff activity, and MoH reporting — no more manual spreadsheets at month-end.
